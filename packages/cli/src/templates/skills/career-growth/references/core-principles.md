# Core Principles (Canonical)

All career-growth drafting, review, and tailoring skills link here and must strictly uphold these principles:

1. **Evidence over Assertion:**
   Every claim, bullet, or evaluation must link directly to verified behaviors, projects, and measurable outcomes cited with valid evidence IDs (`ev-###`). Never invent accomplishments.

2. **The Ledger Ceiling Rule:**
   A technology, language, or system cannot appear on a tailored resume or review unless verified by `evidence/**/*.md` or confirmed by the practitioner. AI tools select, re-weight, and highlight verified facts; they never fabricate capabilities to pass keyword scanners.

3. **Balanced Perspective:**
   Capture both technical strengths and growth areas accurately. Avoid inflated corporate puffery as well as artificial deflation.

4. **Specificity:**
   Include real project context, architectures, dates, and concrete metrics. Reject vague hand-waving and hollow buzzwords. If a quantitative metric is unverified, flag it explicitly with `[METRIC NEEDED]`.

5. **Human Judgment:**
   AI synthesizes, indexes, and drafts. The human engineer or employee owns all submissions, interview representations, self-assessments, and promotion cases. When evidence is missing, flag and stop — do not guess.
