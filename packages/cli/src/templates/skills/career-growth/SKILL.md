## Canonical Principles

All career growth drafting and synthesis tools adhere strictly to [references/core-principles.md](references/core-principles.md):
1. **Evidence over Assertion:** Every bullet and claim must cite verifiable evidence (`ev-###`).
2. **The Ledger Ceiling Rule:** Capabilities not verified by the ledger cannot be added to resumes or reviews.
3. **Balanced Perspective:** Capture real technical challenges and growth opportunities without exaggeration.
4. **Human Judgment:** AI drafts and organizes; the human candidate/engineer owns submissions and career decisions.
